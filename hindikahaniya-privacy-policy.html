1. Information Collection and Use

Hindi Kahaniya does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or photos.

However, the app may use certain third-party services (such as Google AdMob) that automatically collect limited non-personal information required to display advertisements and improve their services.

This information may include:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (derived from IP)
  • App interaction and diagnostic information

2. App Content and Purpose

Hindi Kahaniya provides a collection of Hindi stories across multiple categories. Users can read and listen to stories without registration.

3. Audio Record Permission (Microphone Access)

This application requests Microphone (Audio Record) permission solely for voice command features.

The microphone is used only to:

  • Navigate between stories using voice commands.
  • Control certain app functions through voice.

No audio is recorded, stored, uploaded, or shared. Voice processing occurs locally on the user's device.
